## 1. Information Collection and Processing

> Important Note: This software will not actively upload any of your browsing data to my servers. All browsing-related data is stored locally on your Android device, and I cannot access your browsing history, bookmarks, download history, or other such information.


### 1.1 Locally Stored Information (Saved Only on Your Device Locally)

The following data is stored only on your device locally and will not be automatically uploaded to my servers:

- Browsing history, bookmarks, favorites, download history, web cache, Cookies, and form data.

You can manually clear browsing history, Cookies, and cache at any time in the browser settings.

Cookie Notice: Websites themselves set Cookies for website login and preference memory. This behavior belongs to the respective website and is subject to that website's privacy policy; the browser provides toggles to allow you to block/clear website Cookies.


### 1.2 System Permissions Required by the App

This software only requests permissions essential for implementing its functions. All permissions are controlled by the Android system and will not be obtained silently:

1. Network Permission: A basic and essential permission for accessing the internet; otherwise, web browsing is not possible.

2. Storage Permission: Used only for saving downloaded web files to your device; you can disable storage permission in system settings. After disabling, downloaded files cannot be saved, but basic web browsing is not affected.

3. Location Permission: The software itself does not actively request location permission; only when certain web pages proactively request it will the Android system display a pop-up window to separately ask for your authorization; you may refuse. After refusal, related functions of the corresponding web page will be unavailable, but basic browser usage will not be affected.

4. Microphone Permission: The software itself does not actively request microphone permission; only when certain web pages proactively request it will the Android system display a pop-up window to separately ask for your authorization; you may refuse. After refusal, related functions of the corresponding web page will be unavailable, but basic browser usage will not be affected.

5. Camera Permission: The software itself does not actively request camera permission; only when certain web pages proactively request it will the Android system display a pop-up window to separately ask for your authorization; you may refuse. After refusal, related functions of the corresponding web page will be unavailable, but basic browser usage will not be affected.


> All permissions can be disabled at any time in your phone's system settings. After disabling the corresponding permission, related functions will fail, but basic web browsing will not be affected.


### 1.3 Search Function Description

When you enter keywords in the built-in search box to initiate a search, the search keywords will be sent to the search engine you select to return search results. This search request is processed by the search engine and is subject to the search engine's own privacy policy. I do not collect or save your search keywords.


### 1.4 Information I Do Not Collect

- Do not collect device identifiers, IMEI, Android ID, or other device identification information;

- No crash log reporting, no user behavior analytics;

- No ad tracking, no third-party SDKs obtaining any of your information;

- Do not collect personal identity information such as name and phone number, and no registration or login is required.


## 2. Information Sharing, Transfer, and Public Disclosure

2.1 Since this software does not upload user personal data to my servers, there is in principle no sharing of personal information with third parties.

2.2 Only the following exceptions exist:

1) With your explicit and active consent;

2) When required by national judicial or administrative authorities in accordance with legal procedures (since I do not store your browsing data, I am objectively unable to provide your browsing-related records);

2.3 There is no personal information transfer resulting from business mergers or acquisitions.


## 3. Information Storage and Security

3.1 User browsing data is entirely saved locally on your Android device; I do not have a cloud-based user database.

3.2 You are responsible for safeguarding your mobile device; device loss may lead to leakage of locally saved browser data.

3.3 The retention period of local data is controlled by you: you can clear it at any time in the browser settings; uninstalling the app will generally clear all local browser data.


## 4. Your Rights Regarding Personal Information

In accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law, you enjoy:

1. Right of Deletion: Clear browsing history, cache, and Cookies in browser settings; uninstall the app to clear all local browser data.

2. Right to Withdraw Permission Authorization: Disable storage, location, and other permissions in your phone's system settings.

3. Right to Inquiry: If you have questions about privacy processing, please send an email to: VieBrowser@hotmail.com.


Since I do not store your personal data, I cannot provide services for accessing or exporting cloud data.


## 5. Third-Party Components Description

This app does not integrate any third-party ad SDKs, analytics SDKs, or crash reporting SDKs.

Note: The browser relies on underlying Android system components, including WebView. WebView is provided and updated by the Android system. WebView's data processing rules follow Google's official Android privacy policy and are not within the control of this application.


## 6. Protection of Minors

6.1 VieY Browser is intended for general internet users. Minors should use the internet under the supervision of a guardian, and guardians should provide proper guidance for minors' internet use.

6.2 This software does not actively collect any information from minors. If you discover that a minor's information has been inadvertently retained, please contact me, and I will assist in handling it.
